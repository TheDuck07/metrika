## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for popular retail merchants, market vendors, and neighborhood store owners (*tiendas de barrio*) in Bolivia. The visual identity reflects resilience, warmth, daily work ethic, and community connection. It eliminates unnecessary cognitive load through straightforward visual hierarchies, immediate recognition of operational tasks, and touch targets structured for single-handed counter use in busy store environments.

### Core Attributes
- **Cálido y Cercano:** Grounded in earthy terracotta, warm golden tones, and comforting cream surfaces inspired by traditional Andean adobe brick, warm sunlit storefronts, and market fabric weaves.
- **Inclusivo y Accesible:** Designed for rapid scanning without strain. Generous target regions, oversized currency figures, and immediate visual status tags accommodate diverse digital literacy levels and fluctuating ambient daylight.
- **Rápido y Funcional:** Clear separation between daily actions (sales, purchases, inventory check) with high tactile clarity, requiring minimal taps and zero financial ambiguity.

### Design Movement
**Modern Warm Folk / Tactile Folk-Minimalism:** Combines clean, contemporary structural grids with warm, inviting folk tones. Interfaces avoid sterile cold grays in favor of warm creamy neutrals, thick outlined illustrative icons, and soft rounded contours that feel natural, trustworthy, and welcoming.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional connection to everyday community commerce. High-contrast combinations ensure legibility under harsh glare or inside dimly lit market stalls.

### Primary (`#C85A32` - Terracota Tostado)
The driving action color used for primary navigation points, key confirmation buttons, primary floating action buttons, and dominant brand headers. Deep enough to deliver AAA contrast against cream bases while projecting energy and reliability.

### Secondary (`#F59E0B` - Mostaza Mercado)
Used for secondary focal nodes, revenue highlights, warning badges, and quick-access categories such as regular sales actions. Paired with dark warm text when used as a solid surface.

### Tertiary (`#2F6B55` - Verde Billetes / Dinero)
A balanced forest emerald reflecting positive cash flow, profit tallies, and completed transactions. Avoids electric neon greens in favor of a trustworthy currency tone.

### Accent Soft Blue (`#3B82F6` / `#2563EB`)
Reserved exclusively for analytics breakdowns, secondary categorical distinction in distribution graphs, and informational system highlights.

### Neutrals & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** Soft warm cream (`#FAF5EE`) replaces clinical white, reducing glare during prolonged daily screen use.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card Container):** Ultra-light cream tint (`#FFFDF9`) with delicate terracotta hairline borders (`#EADCCF`).
- **High Dark Neutral:** Rich charcoal espresso (`#262220`) used for body text, numbers, and high-visibility titles, maintaining maximum legibility without harsh black-on-white vibration.
- **Muted Neutral:** Warm clay gray (`#7B716A`) for subheadings, timestamps, unit labels, and secondary hints.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes immediate legibility for users operating mobile devices with single-hand grips in fast-paced retail environments. `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides geometric clarity softened by humanist counters and open apertures.

### Monetary & Numerical Rules
- Financial balances and cash totals must always feature prominent sizing (`currency-hero`), keeping the currency symbol (`Bs.`) distinct yet proportional.
- Decimals must be explicit and legible. Tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be activated across all itemized calculation tables, stock numbers, and receipt rows to prevent column jitter when numbers change.
- Never set essential text below `12px` to protect legibility across varied mobile hardware and screen qualities.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8pt grid anchors all spacing rhythms, guaranteeing muscle-memory consistency. The layout is optimized primarily for vertical mobile screens with fluid single-column execution.

### Mobile Grid Architecture
- **Canvas Margins:** Standardized at `16px` (`margin-mobile`), expanding to `20px` on larger devices. Content spans 4 fluid mobile columns with `12px` gutters.
- **Thumb Zone Safety:** Essential action triggers (new transaction button, quick record button, barcode search) reside strictly within the lower 40% vertical screen zone to accommodate single-thumb reach.
- **Touch Target Threshold:** All tap zones (buttons, list item options, icon counters, date pills) must measure at least `48px` vertically to prevent mis-taps when working briskly at a store counter.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve crisp readability and a grounded feel, visual hierarchy relies on tonal layering and low-contrast outlines rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Layer Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (App Canvas):** Root background bathed in warm cream (`#FAF5EE`).
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules):** Pure warm tinted surface (`#FFFDF9`) framed by a subtle 1px border (`#EADCCF`). This provides distinct boundaries without visual noise.
- **Level 2 (Active Touch & Quick Hub Tiles):** Solid primary or secondary tiles (`#C85A32` or `#F59E0B`) slightly elevated using an ambient, warm tinted glow: `0 4px 14px rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.16)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Bottom Sheets & Persistent Action Bar):** Grounded floating layers bordered with warm hairline trims, paired with an ambient diffuse shadow: `0 -4px 20px rgba(38, 34, 32, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic, accessible rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`). Forms feel solid and friendly without being childish or overly exaggerated.

- **Standard Buttons & Inputs:** `12px` (`0.75rem`) border radius, balancing friendly curves with efficient hit-area rectangular mass.
- **Content Cards & Transaction Containers:** `16px` (`1rem`) corner radius for modular visual softness.
- **Filter Chips & Interactive Pills:** Pill form (`9999px`) to immediately denote non-destructive selectable items (category tags, date filter toggles).
- **Hero Operational Action Button:** Prominent circular shape (`56px` to `64px`) floating dynamically in the bottom tab bar navigation.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Hero Action (e.g., Registrar Venta, Iniciar):** Solid terracotta (`#C85A32`) fill with high-contrast white text (`#FFFFFF`). Min-height `52px`, radius `14px`, font weight `700`. Press state reduces scale slightly (`0.98`) with a subtle darkening to `#B24F2B`.
- **Secondary / Quick Action Tiles (e.g., Ventas, Compras):** Large square/rectangular touch cards (`min-height: 104px`) with bold white vector iconography, solid fills in `#F59E0B` and `#C85A32`, centered typography, and `16px` border radius.
- **Subtle / Ghost Button (e.g., Crear Cuenta, Descargar):** Transparent or light cream fill with a 1.5px terracotta boundary stroke (`#C85A32`) and terracotta text.

### Currency Metric Cards (Ganancia de Hoy / Caja)
- Enclosed modules featuring a distinct border stroke (`1.5px solid #C85A32`).
- Large headline number in bold terracotta (`currency-hero`), flanked by clear Boliviano currency tags (`Bs.`).
- Internal breakdown rows display sales, costs, and expenses with right-aligned tabular numbers for effortless scanning.

### Input Fields
- Generous text and numeric fields (`50px` height) styled with cream fills (`#FFFFFF` or `#FFFDF9`), soft terracotta border lines (`#E2CFC2`), and rounded contours (`12px`).
- Inverted forms for welcome/login screens: solid white outlines (`2px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) overlaid directly on the solid terracotta backdrop with integrated white iconography.

### Chips & Filter Bars
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) horizontal scrolling filter bars for inventory categories (e.g., *Todos*, *Poco Stock*, *Lácteos*, *Refrescos*).
- **Active State:** Solid terracotta fill (`#C85A32`) with white text.
- **Inactive State:** Cream background with fine terracotta border (`#C85A32`) and terracotta text. Minimum height `36px`, padding `8px 16px`.

### Product & History List Items
- Clean stacked rows housed in container cards with thin border separators.
- **Product Rows:** High-aspect square product thumbnail (`52x52px` with `8px` corner radius) on the left, item title and unit price in the center, and remaining stock units highlighted in bold terracotta or warning yellow on the right.
- **Transaction History:** Explicit indicator lines, date group headers with pill-shaped badges, and distinct visual treatment for revenues (`+ Bs.`) versus expenses (`- Bs.`).

### Navigation Bar (Bottom Navigation)
- Clean, fixed lower bar on off-white base (`#FFFDF9`) with 5 core segments: History (*Historial*), Products (*Inventario*), Primary Fast Action (*Registrar/Vender*), Analytics (*Reportes*), and Profile (*Mi Tienda*).
- Center transaction trigger uses an elevated solid terracotta circle (`#C85A32`) with a bold white checkmark/plus icon.
